- (insight) NewIncomingScreen already has working supplier picker + walk-in + quick-add. Missing is any notion of money PAID — documents has total_amount only, no paid_amount/due/is_paid.
- (insight) No payments table, no cash/ledger table, expenses are mock. AP is greenfield.
- (insight) CONTRADICTION in repo: DebtsBySupplierScreen.md says balances DERIVED from unpaid docs minus payments, never hand-edited. Schema stores a balance column. Neither is implemented.
- (insight) Migrations hand-written in migrate.ts (2 places per column), not Drizzle-generated despite drizzle.config
- (technique) started First Principles Thinking
- (idea by user) Debt is not only supplier invoices — late rent etc is debt too
- (decision by user) Derive debt from BOTH expenses and supplier invoices; each carries total_amount + paid_amount
- (insight by coach) paid_amount as a bare number loses payment history and blocks the cash book; must be a cached sum of payment events
- (decision by user) Chose option B: payments as events, not a paid_amount column. Wants cash book in AND out.
- (direction by user) DebtsByCustomer is hidden/deprecated — don't build customer debt (but keep direction column, free later)
- (insight by user) Shop RARELY buys on debt — debt is the exception. So paid field must DEFAULT to full total; debt only when owner lowers it.
- (idea by user) Rejected both manual invoice-picking (error-prone) and bare 'amount to Ahmed' (loses the link)
- (idea by coach) Auto-allocate oldest-first but SHOW the breakdown as a receipt; header/lines idiom mirroring documents/document_lines
- (idea by user) BREAKTHROUGH: point the payment at supplier.id, not documents.id — collapses the allocation table entirely
- (insight by coach) Supplier-level target means per-invoice FIFO breakdown becomes DERIVABLE at read time, not stored. Deterministic because manual picking was already ruled out.
- (idea by user) One cash book storing ALL transactions in and out
- (idea by user) Collapse party_id/expense_id into a single polymorphic ref_id
- (decision by coach) CORRECTION ACCEPTED: cash and debt are separate books. No payments table — transactions IS the cash book; settling supplier debt is just one reason cash moves.
- (decision) Use polymorphic ref_kind + ref_id (house convention, mirrors documents.party_id). Drops party_id/expense_id pair.
- (decision) Cash book must be COMPLETE — needs ref_kind 'owner' (drawings/injection) and 'other', else drawer won't reconcile and owner stops trusting app
- (decision by user) Accounts are real: cash drawer AND InstaPay. account column ships as real, not placeholder.
- (decision) Do NOT auto-net a party who is both supplier and customer — AP and AR stay separate books
- (insight by user) Shop owner req: notes+attachments on purchase AND sale orders (payment proof, InstaPay proof); must store how much paid vs total
- (insight by coach) ERD GAP FOUND: payment proof belongs on the TRANSACTION, not the document. 3 installments = 3 proofs; document.attachments can't say which proof is which payment. Added transactions.attachments.
- (decision) Two kinds of proof, two homes: documents.attachments = proof of GOODS; transactions.attachments = proof of MONEY
- (decision by user) Payment is either cash or not(InstaPay) — account is a binary in v1, no separate method column. Proof field shows only for InstaPay.
- (decision) paid/total shown on screen but NOT stored — derived from transactions. Cache only if slow.
- (question) OPEN: oldest-first FIFO allocation — always right, or does owner sometimes mean a specific newer invoice? Recommend oldest-first, no override in v1.
- (insight) TEST WALKTHROUGH FOUND BUG: walk-in purchase (party_id=null) has no supplier to point payment at. Added ref_kind='purchase' -> documents.id.
- (insight) TEST WALKTHROUGH FOUND AMBIGUITY: 'sales today cash vs instapay' must come from transactions.occurred_at, never documents (no account column). Split-pocket sale = 1 doc, 2 tx rows.
- (decision) Sales invariant: AR deprecated, so a sale MUST be fully settled — sum of its tx must equal doc total, else money vanishes
- (decision by user) Sentinel parties: seed a placeholder supplier AND customer for walk-in instead of null party_id. Supersedes ref_kind='purchase'.
